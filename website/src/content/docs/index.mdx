## Why Vitryla UI

- **Native first.** Dialog, Accordion and Popover are built on the platform: `<dialog>`, `<details>` and the Popover API.
- **Composable.** Components are made of small parts, so you can assemble your own without hacks.
- **Themeable.** Override a few tokens, or switch between light, dark and system themes.
- **Accessible by default.** Labels, descriptions, errors and keyboard support work out of the box.
